## Star Hook Protocol Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026　　Effective date: November 25, 2026

Flat Monster Studio ("we", "us") develops and operates the game Star Hook Protocol (摘星矿工, "the Game"). The Game is a single-player game, and we process as little information as possible. This policy covers the PC version of the Game distributed on Steam, and explains what we collect, why, and how you can turn it off.

### 1. Information we collect

The Game requires no account and has no in-app purchases, ads, social features or push notifications. We do not collect your name, phone number, ID documents, payment details, precise location, contacts, photos or audio.

| Information | Where it is stored | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Save files, settings, run statistics and voyage log | On your computer | Running the game |
| Cloud save sync | Steam Cloud (handled by Valve) | Syncing saves across computers |
| Anonymous gameplay data + random install ID | GameAnalytics | Analysing difficulty and balance |
| Anonymous device hardware information | Unity Technologies | Improving engine compatibility and performance |

"Anonymous gameplay data" means: game launches and play time; for each battle, the floor, room type, score and target score, and result; modules obtained; the outcome, score and build of each run; options chosen in event rooms; plus the game version and operating system type and version. The random install ID is a random string created on your device the first time the Game runs. It only tells devices apart and is not linked to your Steam account or any identity. Analytics servers see your IP address when receiving data and may use it to infer your country or region.

Anonymous gameplay data is sent while Settings → Analytics is on. You can turn it off at any time; the Game remains fully playable.

### 2. How we use it

We only look at aggregated statistics in the analytics dashboards (such as pass rates per floor, difficulty per room, module usage, daily players and retention) to tune difficulty and balance. We run no servers of our own, and we do not sell or rent your information or use it for advertising or profiling. Before using it for any purpose not described here, we will update this policy and ask for your consent.

### 3. Third-party services

| Third party | Purpose | Privacy policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GameAnalytics ApS | Game analytics | [gameanalytics.com/privacy](https://gameanalytics.com/privacy) |
| Valve Corporation (Steam) | Distribution, Steam Cloud saves | [store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement) |
| Unity Technologies | Game engine | [unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) |

Your Steam account and Steam Cloud saves are handled by Valve; we do not receive your Steam profile information. Each third party processes data under its own privacy policy. Apart from the above, your explicit consent, or a legal requirement, we do not share, transfer or disclose your information.

### 4. Storage and international transfers

Local game data is deleted when you delete your saves or uninstall the Game (Steam Cloud saves can be managed in the Steam client). GameAnalytics and Unity data may be stored outside your country (for example in the EU or the US). Retention follows each provider's policy, and we use the data only as long as needed for the purposes above.

### 5. Your rights

- Opt out: turn off Settings → Analytics at any time, effective immediately, or play offline.
- Access and deletion: you can view and delete local data in the Game. Uploaded data is anonymous, so we usually cannot link it to you; if you want it deleted, contact us and we will help as far as we can.
- Players in the EU, UK and similar regions: we process anonymous gameplay data based on our legitimate interest in improving game balance. You can object at any time by turning off Analytics, and you have the right to access, rectify, erase or restrict processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

### 6. Children

If you are a minor (in particular under 14, or under 13 in the US), please use the Game only after a parent or guardian has read and agreed to this policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if a parent or guardian believes we have, please contact us.

### 7. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above; for significant changes (such as new kinds of data or new third parties) we will also notify you in the Game. We will not reduce your rights under this policy without your explicit consent.

### 8. Contact us

We usually reply within fifteen working days.

## 《摘星矿工》隐私政策

更新日期：2026年11月25日　　生效日期：2026年11月25日

平面怪兽工作室（以下简称“我们”）是游戏《摘星矿工》的开发者和运营者。摘星矿工是一款单机游戏，我们尽可能少地处理您的信息。本政策适用于通过 Steam 发行的摘星矿工电脑版，说明我们收集哪些信息、用来做什么、以及您如何关闭。

### 一、我们收集的信息

摘星矿工无需注册账号，不提供内购、广告、社交和推送功能，不会收集您的姓名、手机号、身份证件、支付信息、精确位置、通讯录、照片或录音。

| 信息 | 保存位置 | 用途 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 游戏存档、设置、「本局统计」「航行记录」 | 您的电脑本地 | 游戏运行 |
| 存档云同步 | Steam 云（由 Valve 处理） | 在不同电脑间同步存档 |
| 匿名游玩数据 + 随机安装标识 | GameAnalytics | 分析关卡难度与平衡 |
| 匿名设备硬件信息 | Unity Technologies | 改进引擎兼容性与性能 |

“匿名游玩数据”指：游戏启动与游玩时长；每场战斗所在层数、房间类型、得分与要求分、结果；获得的模组；每局的结局、分数与构筑；事件房中选择的选项；以及游戏版本、操作系统类型与版本。随机安装标识是游戏首次运行时在本机生成的一串随机字符，只用于区分不同设备，不包含、也不关联您的 Steam 账号或任何身份信息。数据分析服务的服务器在接收数据时会看到您的 IP 地址，可能用其推断您所在的国家或地区。

在您未关闭「设置 - 数据统计」时，游戏会上报上述匿名游玩数据；您可以随时关闭，关闭后游戏照常游玩。

### 二、我们如何使用这些信息

我们只在数据分析服务的后台查看汇总统计（如各层通过率、各房间难度、各模组的使用情况、日活与留存），用于调整游戏难度与平衡。我们不自建服务器，不出售、不出租您的信息，不用于广告或用户画像。如需将信息用于本政策未载明的用途，我们会先更新本政策并征得您的同意。

### 三、第三方服务

| 第三方 | 使用目的 | 隐私政策 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GameAnalytics ApS | 游戏数据分析 | [gameanalytics.com/privacy](https://gameanalytics.com/privacy) |
| Valve Corporation（Steam） | 游戏发行、Steam 云存档 | [store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement) |
| Unity Technologies | 游戏引擎运行 | [unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) |

Steam 账号、Steam 云存档由 Valve 处理，我们不会获取您的 Steam 账号资料。上述第三方对信息的具体处理以其各自的隐私政策为准。除上述情形、您的明确同意或法律法规的强制要求外，我们不会共享、转让或公开披露您的信息。

### 四、数据的保存与跨境

本地游戏数据在您删除存档或卸载游戏后即被删除（Steam 云存档可在 Steam 客户端中管理）。GameAnalytics 与 Unity 的数据可能存储于您所在国家或地区以外（如欧盟、美国）。保存期限以各服务方的政策为准，我们只在实现本政策所述目的所需的期限内使用。

### 五、您的权利

- 关闭：随时在「设置 - 数据统计」中关闭上报，立即生效；也可以断开网络游玩。
- 查看与删除：本地数据可在游戏内查看和删除。已上传的数据是匿名的，我们通常无法把它对应到您本人；如您希望删除，请联系我们，我们会尽力协助。
- 欧盟、英国等地区的玩家：我们基于“改进游戏平衡”的正当利益处理匿名游玩数据，您可以随时反对（即关闭数据统计），并享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理及向当地数据保护机构投诉的权利。

### 六、未成年人

如您是未成年人（尤其是不满 14 周岁），请在监护人阅读并同意本政策后使用我们的服务。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息；如监护人发现相关情况，请联系我们处理。

### 七、政策更新

本政策变更时，我们会在本页面发布新版并更新上方日期；重大变更（如新增收集的信息种类或第三方）会在游戏内提示。未经您的明确同意，我们不会削减您按本政策享有的权利。
